# Controlling Senior Manager (CSM) Definition

## Overview

A Controlling Senior Manager (CSM) is an individual who exercises significant control or management authority over a legal entity. CSM identification is a critical component of Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance.

## CSM Criteria

A person should be classified as a **CSM** if they meet ANY of the following criteria:

### Criterion 1: Executive Management Role

The person holds a senior executive position with decision-making authority:

- **Managing Director** (Geschäftsführer)
- **Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**
- **Chief Financial Officer (CFO)**
- **Chief Operating Officer (COO)**
- **General Manager**
- **Board Member** (Vorstand, Aufsichtsrat)
- **Partner** (in partnerships)
- **Sole Proprietor** (Inhaber)

### Criterion 2: Significant Ownership

The person holds significant ownership or beneficial interest:

- **25% or more** ownership/shareholding (direct or indirect)
- **Beneficial owner** with significant economic interest
- **Controlling shareholder**

### Criterion 3: Signing Authority

The person has authority to bind the company legally:

- **Authorized Signatory** (Prokurist) with sole signing authority (Einzelprokura)
- **Power of Attorney** holder with broad business authority
- **Legal Representative** with full representation rights

### Criterion 4: Supervisory Control

The person exercises supervisory control over the company:

- **Chairman of the Board** (Vorstandsvorsitzender)
- **Chairman of the Supervisory Board** (Aufsichtsratsvorsitzender)
- **Controlling person** with veto rights or special voting powers

### Criterion 5: Day-to-Day Control

The person exercises actual day-to-day control over business operations:

- Even without formal title, if they direct business decisions
- Shadow directors or de facto controllers
- Key decision makers identified in company documents

## NON_CSM Classification

A person should be classified as **NON_CSM** if:

1. They do NOT meet any of the CSM criteria above
2. They hold only minor or administrative roles:
   - General employee
   - Administrative staff
   - Junior manager without decision-making authority
   - Minority shareholder (< 25%) without other control indicators
3. They have limited signing authority only (Gesamtprokura - must sign jointly)

## Classification Guidelines

### When in Doubt

If the information is ambiguous:
1. Consider the totality of evidence
2. Look for multiple indicators of control
3. If the person appears to have significant influence, lean toward CSM
4. Document your reasoning clearly

### German Title Mapping

| German Title | Typical Classification | Reasoning |
|--------------|----------------------|-----------|
| Geschäftsführer | CSM | Executive management (Criterion 1) |
| Vorstand | CSM | Board member (Criterion 1) |
| Vorstandsvorsitzender | CSM | Chairman (Criterion 4) |
| Aufsichtsrat | CSM | Supervisory control (Criterion 4) |
| Prokurist (Einzelprokura) | CSM | Sole signing authority (Criterion 3) |
| Prokurist (Gesamtprokura) | NON_CSM | Limited authority only |
| Gesellschafter (≥25%) | CSM | Significant ownership (Criterion 2) |
| Gesellschafter (<25%) | NON_CSM | Minor ownership |
| Liquidator | CSM | Full control during liquidation |
| Komplementär | CSM | Unlimited liability partner |
| Kommanditist | Depends | CSM if ≥25%, otherwise NON_CSM |

### Evidence Requirements

When classifying a person, document:
1. Which criterion/criteria they meet (or don't meet)
2. The specific evidence supporting the classification
3. The source document and page where evidence was found
4. Any uncertainties or assumptions made
